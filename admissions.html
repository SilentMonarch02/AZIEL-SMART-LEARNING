Admissions

How to apply

1
Send an enquiry

Contact the office or fill in the form below with your child's age and the level you are interested in.

2
Visit the school

Tour the campus, meet the teachers and ask your questions.

3
Submit the application

Return the completed admission form with the required documents.

4
Assessment and placement

Your child takes a short, friendly assessment so we can place them well.

5
Confirm the place

Receive your offer, complete registration and get ready for the first day.

Documents you will need

Birth certificate copy, passport photos of the learner, previous school report or transfer letter where applicable, and parent or guardian ID copy.

Which levels are open?

Early Years (PP1–PP2), Lower Primary, Upper Primary and Junior School. Contact the office to confirm current availability.

Fees and payment

Fees depend on the level. Request the current fee structure from the office and we will send it to you.

Can I visit before applying?

Yes. Book a visit and we will show you around during school hours.
