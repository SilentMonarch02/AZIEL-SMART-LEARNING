Who we are

About The Aziel Smart Academy

A school built on knowledge, character and technology

The Aziel Smart Academy is a Kenyan school committed to helping every learner grow academically and personally. Our emblem, an open book beneath a graduation cap, sits inside a shield: learning protected and passed on.

Aziel Smart Learning is our online home. Here parents find school information, and learners find the digital resources that support what happens in class.

Our Mission

To give every learner a strong foundation in knowledge, skills and values, using smart teaching methods that prepare them for a changing world.

Our Vision

Confident, capable and responsible young Kenyans who learn for life and lead with integrity.

Our Values

Excellence

We set high standards and give learners the support to reach them.

Integrity

Honesty and respect guide how we teach, lead and behave.

Innovation

We use smart tools thoughtfully so learning stays human and engaging.

Community

School, family and community share the work of raising each child.
